Your face tells a story

Stress, tension and ageing can change how your face looks and feels.

I look beyond the surface, working with facial muscles, connective tissue and lymphatic flow to support natural facial definition, release tension and restore radiance.

Who is it for?

For women who…

  • Notice facial puffiness, tiredness or loss of definition.
  • Want a more sculpted, refreshed appearance.
  • Carry tension around their jaw and face.
  • Want to age naturally without invasive treatments.
  • Believe beauty is about feeling good as well as looking good.

Signature treatment

Buccal massage

With gloved hands, Ami works the cheek and jaw muscles from inside the mouth as well as outside, reaching tension a standard facial can't. It is done entirely by hand and shaped around your face.

  • Releases held tension in the jaw and cheeks
  • Supports natural-looking contours and definition
  • Non-invasive, with no needles

A little about Ami

Hello, I'm Ami.

My passion for facial sculpting began with my own experience of ageing. I wanted to understand why our faces change and how we can support them naturally.

With a background in Cosmetic Science and advanced facial sculpting training, I combine knowledge with specialised hands-on techniques to help women reconnect with their natural beauty.

I believe beauty goes beyond the surface. It's about how you look, how you feel and how you care for yourself.
