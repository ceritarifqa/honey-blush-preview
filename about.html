About me

Hello, I'm Ami.

A mum of two, a beauty enthusiast and the founder of Honey Blush Beauty.

My passion for beauty goes beyond what we see on the surface. I believe looking and feeling good is about more than simply treating our skin or chasing a youthful appearance. It's about understanding our face and body, caring for ourselves and embracing a more holistic approach to beauty.

My story

My journey into beauty

My journey began with studying Cosmetic Science and Cosmetic Brand Management, where I developed an understanding of the science behind beauty, skin and cosmetic products.

But my curiosity didn't stop there. I wanted to understand more about the face and body, how they work and how specialised treatments could support natural-looking results and overall wellbeing.

This passion led me to pursue further education in facial and body sculpting treatments, continually investing in my knowledge and practical skills.

I believe learning never stops. Every new technique and every opportunity to expand my knowledge helps me become a better therapist and provide more thoughtful, personalised treatments for my clients.

My training includes

  • Cosmetic Science

  • Cosmetic Brand Management

  • Body Sculpting training with Gingerella Rox

  • Osteopathic Facial Sculpting Massage training

  • European Sculptural Massage Techniques

  • Advanced Facial Sculpting and Buccal Massage training with Isabella Loneragan at Skinformé Academy

My philosophy

Beauty beyond the surface

Your face doesn't exist in isolation. And your body deserves the same understanding.

I believe beauty is about more than treating what we see on the surface.

Our face and body are connected to our overall wellbeing. Facial and body muscles, connective tissue, lymphatic flow, posture and everyday tension can all influence how we look and feel.

That's why my approach goes beyond traditional beauty treatments.

For the face

I focus on specialised facial sculpting massage, buccal techniques, lymphatic drainage and facial tension release to support natural-looking contours and a refreshed, radiant appearance.

For the body

I combine targeted body sculpting techniques and skin-tightening technologies to help improve the appearance of body contours and skin firmness.

My goal is to understand your individual concerns and choose treatments that support your desired outcomes, rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach.

I believe beauty isn't about chasing perfection or relying on quick fixes. It's about understanding your body, caring for yourself and embracing the natural ageing journey.

Don't just treat the skin. Understand the face. Understand the body.

Because true beauty is not about changing who you are. It's about understanding, nurturing and bringing out the best in what you already have.

My approach

At Honey Blush Beauty, every client is treated as an individual.

I take the time to understand your concerns, listen to what you want to achieve and tailor treatments to suit your needs.

Whether you're looking to refresh tired-looking skin, address facial puffiness, release facial tension, enhance your natural facial contours, improve the appearance of stubborn body areas or support firmer-looking skin, my approach is personal, considered and focused on your individual goals.

I want every visit to be more than just a treatment. It's your time to slow down, reconnect with yourself and feel cared for.

I look forward to seeing you.

With love, Ami Amalia
