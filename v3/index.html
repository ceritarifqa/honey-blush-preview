Why buccal

Most clinics on the Gold Coast lift with machines. Ami lifts with her hands.

Buccal facial sculpting is an intra-oral facial massage. Wearing gloves, Ami massages the muscles of the cheeks and jaw from inside the mouth, then works the same muscles from outside. As the tension lets go, the face sits higher and the jawline looks more defined, without needles.

  • Done by hand, with every movement adjusted to your face.
  • Worked from inside the mouth and outside, reaching muscles a standard facial cannot.
  • Ami trained with a pioneering buccal practitioner in Sydney.
  • Manual buccal sculpting is still rare on the Gold Coast.

Meet Ami

Ami built Honey Blush Beauty on her own, as a private studio at her home in Currumbin Waters, a few minutes from the beach. She sees every client herself, so each treatment is shaped around your face and what you want from it. She trained in buccal sculpting with a pioneering practitioner in Sydney and brought the technique home to the Gold Coast, where few studios offer it by hand.
